# Getting Started

<details>
<summary>What is goodWill and how does it work?</summary>

goodWill is a blood donation platform that connects donors, patients, hospitals, and blood banks. It helps users find blood resources quickly during emergencies and rewards donors for their contributions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need to create an account to use the platform?</summary>

Donors can quickly access the platform using the "Continue as Donor" option. Hospitals use a separate login system with email and password credentials.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the system requirements?</summary>

A modern web browser and an internet connection are recommended for the best experience.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which browsers are supported?</summary>

The platform is designed to work on modern browsers such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, and Safari.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is my data safe and private?</summary>

goodWill is designed to handle user information responsibly. Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information and follow platform guidelines.

</details>

# Blood Donation Process

<details>
<summary>How do I register as a donor?</summary>

Donors can quickly access the platform using the "Continue as Donor" option and proceed to the donation workflow.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What information do I need to provide?</summary>

The platform may require donor and donation-related information to help process donations and maintain accurate records.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How does the donation matching work?</summary>

goodWill helps connect blood requests with compatible donors and available blood resources to improve response times during emergencies.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I donate multiple times?</summary>

Yes, eligible donors can donate multiple times. Donors should follow local medical guidelines regarding donation frequency.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens after I complete a donation?</summary>

Your donation is recorded, hospital blood inventory may be updated, and you earn 15 reward points for a successful donation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is my location needed?</summary>

Location information helps the platform identify nearby hospitals, blood banks, and potential donor matches more efficiently.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does a donation take?</summary>

The actual donation usually takes around 8–10 minutes, while the complete process generally takes less than one hour.

</details>

</details>

<details>
<summary>How many lives can one donation help?</summary>

A single blood donation may help up to three patients because donated blood can be separated into different components.

</details>

# Blood Type Compatibility

<details>
<summary>Why does blood type matter?</summary>

Blood type compatibility helps ensure safe blood transfusions and reduces the risk of adverse reactions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the different blood types?</summary>

The major blood groups are A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, and O-.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which blood types are universal donors and recipients?</summary>

O- is commonly known as the universal donor type, while AB+ is considered the universal recipient type.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I donate if I have a rare blood type?</summary>

Yes. Rare blood types are often in high demand and can be extremely valuable during emergencies.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find my blood type?</summary>

You can learn your blood type through previous medical records, blood donation history, or a blood test performed by a healthcare provider.

</details>

# Reward System

<details>
<summary>How many points do I earn per donation?</summary>

You earn 15 reward points for each successful blood donation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I redeem my points?</summary>

Visit the Redeem section of the platform and select an available reward if you have enough points.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What rewards are available?</summary>

Examples include ORS Solution, Bandages, Paracetamol, Antiseptic Solution, Vitamin Supplements, Digital Thermometers, First Aid Kits, and Blood Pressure Monitors.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How many points do rewards cost?</summary>

Reward costs vary. Some items start at 30 points, while larger rewards may require up to 300 points.

</details>

# Hospitals & Blood Banks

<details>
<summary>How are hospitals listed on the platform?</summary>

Hospitals are maintained within the platform's database and can be viewed along with their available blood inventory information.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I see real-time blood availability?</summary>

The platform displays hospital blood stock information to help users identify available blood resources and shortages.

</details>

<summary>How do I request blood from a hospital?</summary>

Navigate to the Request Blood section, provide the required patient details, and submit your request. The platform helps connect requests with available resources and compatible donors.

</details>

Blood inventory levels vary between hospitals. Users can view hospital blood stock information to identify which blood types are currently needed or available.

# Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>The app won't load, what should I do?</summary>

Try refreshing the page, checking your internet connection, and using a supported browser. If the problem persists, restart the application and verify that the required services are running.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Location detection isn't working</summary>

Ensure that location permissions are enabled in your browser and device settings. You may also need to allow location access when prompted by the application.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't log in to my account</summary>

Verify that your login credentials are correct. Hospital users should ensure they are using the correct email and password. If the issue continues, contact the project maintainers.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The donation form has errors</summary>

Check that all required fields have been completed correctly and that the submitted information follows the expected format. Refresh the page and try again if the issue persists.

</details>
