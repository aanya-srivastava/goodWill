# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to the goodWill FAQ! Here you will find answers to the most common questions about using our platform, the reward system, and the donation process.

## Getting Started

### What is goodWill and how does it work?
goodWill is a platform dedicated to connecting blood donors with those in need. Users can register, find nearby hospitals or blood banks, and participate in a community-driven blood donation ecosystem.

### Do I need to create an account to use the platform?
Yes, creating an account is highly recommended. It allows you to track your donations, earn rewards, and receive personalized notifications.

### What are the system requirements?
goodWill is a web-based platform. You only need an active internet connection and a modern device (smartphone, tablet, or PC).

### Which browsers are supported?
We support all modern web browsers, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge.

## Donations & Rewards

### What is the step-by-step donation process?
1. Create an account and complete your profile.
2. Browse active donation requests or nearby hospital needs.
3. Schedule an appointment or visit the center.
4. Update your donation status on the platform to claim points.

### How does the reward system work?
We appreciate our donors! You earn **15 points per donation**. These points accumulate in your account and can be redeemed through our reward mechanics.

### How do I check blood type compatibility?
A universal blood donor (O-) can donate to anyone, while an AB+ recipient can receive from anyone. We ensure that donation requests automatically match with compatible donor profiles.
